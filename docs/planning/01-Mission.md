# Mission and product positioning

Almost Heard. For the music that hasn't found its audience yet.

Help independent creators, including AI music creators, find listeners. Do not attempt to replace DistroKid, Spotify or other streaming services. Distribution makes music available; Almost Heard aims to make it discoverable. External streaming links are encouraged; a listener following an artist elsewhere is a successful outcome.

## Product ecosystem
- Creators Dashboard: control and organize music.
- Public Creators site: professional artist, release and song pages and web playback.
- Almost Heard Player: personal listening for members and curated public discovery.
- Weekly promotions: earned visibility, not guaranteed distribution.
- Established Creator recognition: reward quality and contribution.
- Promotion Board: eventually give trusted creators a voice in curation.

The first three deliver standalone subscription value before promotions exist. A curated public catalogue of hundreds rather than millions is the intended differentiation, not a hard numerical limit. No promises of fame or guaranteed promotion.

## Growth loop
Creators install the player to enjoy their own catalogue, naturally encountering Almost Heard Originals. As guest recordings earn promotion, creators have a reason to share the player with their followers. This can build an audience for both Originals and guest creators. Exposure should be organic, not forced listening or inflated engagement.

## Product boundaries
Main almostheard.ca remains the Originals site. creators.almostheard.ca hosts guest creator pages. artists.almostheard.ca is the fictional Canon, not a guest feature. The mobile player eventually serves Originals and promoted guest recordings, plus each connected creator's private library. Catalogue classifications distinguish these sources.
