# Membership and lifecycle

## Proposed pricing and allowances
Canadian dollars: $5/month or $50/year. Proposed base allowance: 3 artists, 300 songs, 30 releases. Established Creator allowance: 5 artists, 600 songs, 60 releases. Both: maximum 5 new song publications/day and 50/month. Deletion and republication must not bypass quotas. Pricing and numeric limits remain proposals.

One creator account can own multiple artist identities. Active membership provides dashboard, public presence and private mobile listening. Public mobile promotion is earned separately.

## Cancellation (agreed direction)
Cancellation immediately removes public creator-site presence and private player authorization, revoking all device connections. Publicly promoted songs normally leave the public player at the next Friday update, but selected quality recordings may remain indefinitely with creator consent, available media and continued engagement. Creator may withdraw consent at any time.

Retain catalogue metadata for 60 days, read-only; then delete unpromoted metadata. Retained promoted records need sufficient metadata to operate. Offer metadata export (JSON/CSV proposed). Creator owns media in their own R2 account, avoiding media lock-in.

Reactivation within the retention window requires payment for intervening months to restore catalogue and privileges, or wait until deletion and start fresh. No automatic retroactive charge. New device pairing required. Billing mechanics, annual-plan treatment and exact cancellation timing relative to paid-through periods need definition; immediate loss of private connection on cancellation is the agreed product rule.

## Founding beta
Invite five active quality AI creators for three complimentary months once the core package is ready. Gather feedback and genuine engagement before setting promotion thresholds. No guarantee of promotion.
