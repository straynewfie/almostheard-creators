# Engagement analytics and weekly promotion

Track qualified plays, unique likes and endorsements with privacy-conscious aggregation. Distinguish total legitimate plays, independent audience plays and weighted promotion score. Almost Heard owns centralized analytics; private and public player sources report authenticated context.

## Self-listening (agreed direction)
Legitimate creator self-plays may count at reduced weight. Illustrative beta values: independent play 1.0, self-play 0.1, self-listening capped at 10% of promotion score. These values are not final. Classify by recording ownership and creator identity, not merely private/public surface. Apply qualification, repeat-play limits and fraud controls server-side. Exact cap formula, thresholds and treatment of self-likes remain open.

## Promotion
Website publication does not guarantee public mobile inclusion. Friday promotion batch and public announcement; qualify using genuine engagement and editorial selection initially, later board votes. Thresholds and automated rules TBD. Share cards and badges support creator outreach. Promotion belongs to exact recording; audio replacement resets eligibility and promotion (see 04).

## Abuse prevention
Detect duplicate/bot engagement, reciprocal or circular manipulation, cliques, related accounts and concentrated voting. Flags are not proof. Confirmed violations may suspend eligibility, remove promoted recordings, revoke Established Creator or board privileges and reverse fraudulent engagement. Maintain audit trail, review and appeal; avoid routine manual review where feasible.

## Public retention
Cancellation normally removes promoted recordings next Friday, subject to selected-recording retention with consent, media availability and engagement. Immediate withdrawal remains available. Private access ends immediately regardless.
