# Established Creators, Promotion Board and Promotion Coins

Future phases, not launch dependencies. Initially owner handles promotion, optionally with trusted reviewers. Board replaces the earlier individual sponsorship/endorsement concept as the intended governance model; reconcile older issue wording before implementation.

## Established Creator
Earned recognition and expanded catalogue allowances (see 02). Criteria TBD; not purchasable. Abuse can revoke status.

## Board membership
Monthly invite to top 10% of eligible creators (proposed percentage), voluntary participation; board size grows with creator population. Creator performance earns invitation; board performance earns continued seat. Falling outside top 10% does not automatically remove a reliable member; poor board performance can lead to replacement even for a top creator. Define ranking, minimum size and reliability criteria later.

## Weekly process
Monday reveal shared candidate pool. Members listen to N songs and vote for top M; equal votes, private ballots, no voting for own account. Voting closes before Friday. Approval thresholds, quorum, N and M TBD. Friday promotions and announcement.

Members completing duties earn one Promotion Coin Friday. During weekend, optionally redeem for one qualified own song to be considered next week, not guaranteed promotion. Unused coin expires Monday. No accumulation, transfer or purchase. No penalty for not redeeming. Track reliability by coins earned versus opportunities, not redemption.

## Integrity
Monitor reciprocal/circular voting, cliques, related accounts and concentration. Investigate flags rather than treating them as proof. Confirmed abuse may revoke seats and privileges or reverse fraudulent outcomes. Establish audit and appeal processes.
