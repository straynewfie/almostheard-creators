Our mission

For the music that hasn't found its audience yet.

Millions of songs compete for attention. Making music available is no longer the biggest challenge. Getting people to discover it is.

Why we're building this

Music deserves the chance to be heard.

Almost Heard exists to help independent music creators get heard. We aren't here to replace your creative tools, your distributor or your favourite streaming service. We're here to complement them: helping you establish your musical identity, present your catalogue, reach new listeners and, eventually, earn additional exposure through a curated listening platform.

We want to give the little guy a better chance of being heard.

Your music. Your storage. Your catalogue. Your audience.
One mission. Connected tools.

The Almost Heard ecosystem

Each part has its own purpose. Together, they help creators move from making music to finding people who want to hear it.

01 · FOUNDATION

Creator Dashboard

Manage multiple artist identities, organize songs into releases, and upload music and artwork to cloud storage you own.

02 · PUBLIC PRESENCE

Creators Website

Give your music a shareable home, with artist pages, discographies, browser playback and a way to discover other creators.

03 · DISCOVERY

Almost Heard Player

A dedicated listening experience built around a smaller, curated catalogue where emerging music has room to stand out.

04 · OPPORTUNITY

Weekly Promotions

Give qualifying recordings a route into the player through genuine engagement, editorial selection and, eventually, community review.

05 · RECOGNITION

Creator Achievements

Recognize consistent quality and meaningful listener response, rather than simply rewarding publishing volume.

06 · COMMUNITY

Promotion Board

Invite proven creators to help champion overlooked music, with earned responsibilities and rewards.

Beyond our walls

Discovery shouldn't end here.

When someone discovers your music through Almost Heard and follows you on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music or another service, that's a success. We want to help grow your audience everywhere, not lock it into one platform.

Building toward the mission

A platform that grows in stages

Almost Heard Creators is in development. The following features describe our vision, not services available today.

Stage 1
Creator Dashboard and public website. Own, organize, publish and share your catalogue.
Stage 2
Player integration. Introduce a curated listening audience.
Stage 3
Weekly promotions. Give deserving recordings an opportunity for greater visibility.
Stage 4
Creator recognition. Celebrate quality and contribution.
Stage 5
Promotion Board. Bring trusted creators into community curation and later introduce earned board rewards.
What we promise

Opportunity, not guarantees.

We don't promise fame, guaranteed promotion, thousands of listeners or commercial success. We aim to provide a place to present your music, retain ownership of your media and have an opportunity to reach listeners who might otherwise never find you.
